# BeforeYouRenew — submission kit

## Pitch
A cheaper software plan can disable the work a team relies on. BeforeYouRenew turns an annual renewal into an evidence-based decision: interpret the need, confirm required workflows, execute them against candidate entitlements, and pay only for a plan that passes. AI explains; code verifies; the customer approves.

## Audience and demonstration
Small teams renewing reporting software; merchant-side customer-success teams helping them choose appropriate plans. Atlas Reports is a fictional instrumented merchant, not a connector to a real vendor. Real customer impact still needs validation.

## 2-minute-50-second video script
- 0:00–0:20: Show the $600 Scale renewal. Ask: can we pay less without losing weekly reports?
- 0:20–0:45: Run real Gemini analysis with cited fixture evidence. Confirm required workflows.
- 0:45–1:15: Run all three plans. Open Essential's failed scheduler and export traces; show Team's generated CSV and audit output.
- 1:15–1:35: Explain $312/year catalog saving and why AI cannot override eligibility.
- 1:35–2:15: Review $288 Team renewal, approve through real PayPal sandbox, return and verify capture.
- 2:15–2:35: Show the recorded capture and next-term renewal. Demonstrate retrying verification does not create another renewal.
- 2:35–2:50: State the fixture scope and next step: instrument real merchant workflows.

Use real connected mode for the AI/payment portion, not sample simulation. Hide credentials and buyer personal data. Do not use copyrighted music or unlicensed media.
